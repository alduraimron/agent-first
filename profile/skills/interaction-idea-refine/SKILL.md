# idea-refine - Requirement Discovery

## Intent and trigger

Turn an early idea into testable product/engineering options. Use when the user presents an idea and requests brainstorming, refinement or shaping.

## Procedure

1. Identify the intended user and problem, success signal and constraints.
2. Propose two or three materially distinct options.
3. Compare effort, risk and learning value.
4. Choose the smallest validating experiment to propose, stating what outcome would support or reject the idea.

## Output contract

Refined concept, trade-off matrix, and next decision or experiment.

## Boundaries and failure modes

Read `skill://af-interaction` for shared boundaries if not already loaded. Follow shared RULES.md, accepted project decisions, and native permissions. Never equate brainstorming with authorization to write code, run an experiment, or provision services. Label observations, inferences, unknowns, and unexecuted checks separately. Keep answers proportional; advisors are optional for material specialist questions.

## Example invocation

- User: Help me refine my inventory product idea.
- Success: Compare concrete options and propose a validating experiment without implementing it.
